WELCOME TO THE LARGEST CONTINENT-ASIA

Asia is the largest and most populous continent on Earth, covering about 30% of the world’s land area. It comprises 49 countries, including major nations such as China, India, Japan, and Russia. With over 4.7 billion inhabitants, it is home to more than half of the global population. Asia is remarkably diverse, featuring a wide range of languages, cultures, and traditions. It is the birthplace of several major world religions, including Hinduism, Buddhism, and Islam. Geographically, Asia spans from the Arctic in the north to the Indian Ocean in the south, offering vast natural and cultural variety.
